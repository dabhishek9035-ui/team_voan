# Honest limits (say these before the judges find them)

- A screenshot is a claim, not proof.
- "Travelled" is the traveller's own statement; we cannot see gate scans.
- We cannot issue refunds or confirm the operator's records.
- Corroboration shows consistency, not truth; two fabricated items can agree.
- The adaptive evidence challenge raises the cost of a fake but does not end it.
- Results hold only on data that resembles what we calibrated on (show B's drift check).
- Facts about the metro bot come from our own Day-0 test and a small sample.
- The chain gives tamper evidence, not tamper prevention. It is not a blockchain.
- The synthetic corpus is 30 hand-authored, much cleaner items; it shows the plumbing and edge cases, not real-world accuracy.
- Perceptual hashes alone confuse unrelated screens that share a template; we only use them next to an ID match.
- The two "fake" samples are hand-made mocks, not AI-generator output; AI-fake resistance is untested.
