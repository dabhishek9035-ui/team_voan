# C1: Interview guide (coordinator and traveller)

Goal: prove where the process breaks, in the interviewee's words. Aim for 1 coordinator minimum, 3 if possible.
If none is available: any group payer (event organiser, trek leader, college club treasurer). Label results "small sample".

## Before you start
- Ask consent to take notes and to quote them anonymously. Never record names or phone numbers.
- Do not mention Pratibimb's solution until the end. Do not lead.

## Coordinator questions (from the brief)
1. How many ticket or payment screenshots do you handle per week?
2. How many hours per week do you spend checking and chasing?
3. When did something last go missing, get reused or turn out wrong? What did it cost?
4. How do you find an old screenshot? What happens at handover?
5. When you doubt a screenshot, what do you actually do?
6. Which hurts most: knowing who has a ticket, handling failed payments, or checking claims?

## Traveller questions (3 to 5 people)
1. Has a payment ever gone through with no ticket? How often?
2. How long did the refund take? Did it arrive at all?
3. What did you send, to whom, and how many times?
4. In which language was the message you got?
