# C7: Demo script (about 3 minutes)

Backup video recorded Day 6. Keep one browser tab on the dashboard in fixture mode as a no-network fallback.

| Step | Time | Show | Say |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. The mess | 0:00 | A chat of mixed screenshots: a duplicate, a payment with no ticket, an out-of-window ride | "This is what a coordinator scrolls today." |
| 2. Drop it in | 0:30 | Roster fills as screenshots are processed | "People keep screenshotting. Nothing changes for them." |
| 3. Inbox | 0:55 | Headline "N of M need review"; each row has a reason, relationship type, evidence level | "Most screenshots balance. These do not, and here is why." |
| 4. Trail | 1:25 | One item: Evidence, Interpretation, Relationship, Decision panels | "We separate what was sent, what we read, why we linked it, and who decided." |
| 5. Judge's turn | 1:50 | A judge sends any screenshot live | Classified and linked on screen; show the processing state if slow |
| 6. Break me | 2:15 | A fake triggers an evidence challenge; scoreboard visible | "We don't claim it's true. We ask for more and say how well it's supported." |
| 7. Tamper | 2:40 | Audit view: edit a past row, chain breaks at that row | "Nothing is silently overwritten." |

Opening and closing line:
> "Pratibimb doesn't claim a screenshot is true. It gives every screenshot an owner, a trail and an honest label for how well it's supported, in the language the traveller uses."

Use the real N and M from the run you are showing. Do not say "3 of 40" unless that is what the screen shows.
