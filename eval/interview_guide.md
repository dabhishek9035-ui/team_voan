# Coordinator and traveller interview guide

This is a note-taking guide, not a record of interviews. Fill it only after speaking with real participants and with their permission. Do not write names or contact details in the repository.

## Opening

Explain that the discussion is about how a coordinator handles travel tickets, payment issues and reimbursement screenshots. Participation is voluntary. Ask before taking notes or quoting anything. Explain that screenshots and personal details are not needed for the interview; if examples are offered, do not copy or share them without separate consent.

## Coordinator questions

1. How many ticket or payment screenshots do you handle in a typical week?
2. How much time do you spend checking claims and following up?
3. What was the last case where an item was missing, reused or incorrect? What happened next?
4. How do you find an old screenshot, and what happens when responsibility is handed to someone else?
5. When you doubt a screenshot, what do you do today?
6. Which causes the most work: knowing who has a ticket, handling failed payments, or checking reimbursement claims?

Record answers as anonymized notes. Mark estimates as estimates and direct quotes only when the participant approved quoting.

## Traveller questions

1. What do you do when money appears debited but no ticket arrives?
2. How long did the last refund take, if you have experienced one?
3. Who did you send screenshots to, and how many follow-ups were needed?

Do not infer a frequency from a few interviews. Report the sample size and describe comments as interview findings, not population rates.

## Manual baseline

Use the same labelled cases for the manual and dashboard-assisted passes. Explain the task the same way each time. Start the timer when the case is shown; stop when the coordinator has recorded the disposition and any follow-up. Record errors and missed duplicates separately. Do not run the baseline on names or account details that are unnecessary for the task.

No interview or timing values are currently recorded in this repository.
